The FAFSA allows US citizens and eligible non-citizens to apply for federal grants, work-study funds, and student loans. Ensure LACC federal school code 001223 is included on your application.

Apply on StudentAid.gov

The CADAA allows eligible undocumented and AB 540 students to apply for California state financial aid, including Cal Grants and the California College Promise Grant.

Start CADAA Application

For eligible California residents, the CCPG waives enrollment fees ($46/unit). Applicants should complete either the FAFSA or CADAA to automatically trigger evaluation.

If you have experienced extenuating circumstances or need to appeal your Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) status, submit an appeal form along with required supporting documentation.
